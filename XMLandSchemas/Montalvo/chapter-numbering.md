# Chapter Numbering Note


Although the physical copies of Montalvo's Spanish original and Southey's English translation align, the chapter numbering in our folder naming system differs by one chapter.**Southey's chapter numbers are one higher than Montalvo's.**

**Example:** in folder /XMLandSchemas

```
Montalvo_Amadis_1547_1.xml = Southey_Amadis_1803_2.xml
```

When matching files, account for this one-chapter offset.
